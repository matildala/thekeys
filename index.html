The Book

The Keys is a captivating sci-fi fantasy novel that follows the extraordinary journey of an ordinary protagonist who stumbles upon a mysterious, ornate wooden box hidden in an old attic. Inside lies a collection of intricate, ancient keys—each holding the uncanny power to unlock a doorway into a completely different universe.

The Author

Matilda La is a speculative fiction author known for weaving rich, immersive worlds that blend grounded human emotion with sprawling cosmic wonder. Fascinated by the concepts of parallel dimensions, forgotten history, and high-concept fantasy, Matilda crafts stories that invite readers to look beyond the ordinary and question what lies just out of sight.

The Chapters

Chapter I: The Discovery

A dusty attic reveal brings the mysterious, carved wooden chest and its strange collection of keys into the light.

Chapter II: Echoes of a Past Life

The ornate sapphire key reveals a portal to a grand, bygone era of opulent ballrooms and hidden historical secrets.

Chapter III: The Techno-Crucible

Turning the metallic key opens a gateway into a sprawling, hyper-advanced metropolis governed by high technology.

Chapter IV: Wilderness Trials

The rustic wooden key transports the traveler to a untamed, prehistoric jungle world teeming with ancient creatures.

Chapter V: The Celestial Alignment

A glowing star-tipped key unlocks a starry realm of floating castles and cosmic magic.

Chapter VI: The Gateway Master

With all portals swirling at once, the ultimate choice must be made to balance the multiverse or risk closing the doors forever.
